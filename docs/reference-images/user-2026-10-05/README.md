# Mecha reference pack — 2026-10-05

These three user-supplied images are private visual references for the future robot-jigsaw system. They are not production assets and must not be displayed in the game.

## Useful design signals

### 01 — red/black heavy mecha

- large armored silhouette and broad shoulders;
- layered plates over visible mechanical structure;
- strong central energy core and asymmetrical equipment;
- useful direction for an original **Titan-class** robot.

### 02 — blue/red vehicle-armored mecha

- recognizable vehicle parts integrated into limbs and torso;
- dense mechanical detail with a heroic, upright stance;
- large melee equipment and reinforced lower legs;
- useful direction for an original **Guardian- or Titan-class** robot.

### 03 — yellow compact mecha

- rounded vehicle panels, smaller proportions and agile pose;
- bright single-color identity with dark mechanical joints;
- friendly silhouette suitable for younger students;
- useful direction for an original **Scout-class** robot.

## Originality constraints

- Do not copy the Transformers title, faction logos, character faces, exact color layouts, vehicle transformations or signature weapons.
- New robots need original names, silhouettes, head shapes, armor seams, reactors and weapon systems.
- Preserve only broad genre qualities: layered mechanical armor, visible joints, vehicle-inspired panels, scale and weight.
- Final art should remain readable when divided into jigsaw pieces and when shown as a small inventory thumbnail.

## Proposed first original trio

1. **Kite-7** — compact amber Scout; scanner wings and a magnetic repair tool.
2. **Bastion-R** — red/graphite Guardian; wide shield shoulders and a pulse cannon.
3. **Atlas Nova** — navy/copper Titan; layered orbital armor and a detachable engine lance.

These names and descriptions are working concepts only. The final visual generation begins after the owner chooses the first class to build.
